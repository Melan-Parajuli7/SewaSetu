# SewaSetu

**SewaSetu** is a full-stack MERN web application that connects people with trusted local service providers in Nepal.  
Users can find professionals such as plumbers, electricians, cleaners, tutors, painters, and technicians, view their profiles, book services, and leave reviews.

> **Sewa** means *service*, and **Setu** means *bridge*.  
> SewaSetu acts as a digital bridge between customers and local service providers.

---

## 🌏 What is SewaSetu?

Finding a reliable plumber, electrician, cleaner, tutor, or repair technician can be difficult. People often depend on word of mouth, phone calls, or social media posts, which makes it hard to compare prices, check experience, or trust the service provider.

**SewaSetu solves this problem by providing one online platform where:**

- Customers can discover local service providers.
- Providers can create professional profiles and offer their services.
- Customers can compare services, prices, ratings, and reviews.
- Users can book a service online instead of searching manually.
- Service providers can receive booking requests and manage their work.

---

## 🤝 How SewaSetu Helps People

### For Customers

SewaSetu helps customers find the right service provider quickly and safely.

- Search for services by category, location, price, and rating.
- View provider profiles, skills, experience, and customer reviews.
- Book a service by selecting a date, time, and location.
- Track booking status from **pending** to **accepted** and **completed**.
- Rate and review service providers after the work is completed.
- Save favorite providers for future use.

### For Service Providers

SewaSetu helps skilled workers and small service businesses reach more customers online.

- Create a professional service profile.
- Add services, pricing, availability, and service areas.
- Receive and manage booking requests.
- Build trust through verified profiles, ratings, and reviews.
- Manage completed jobs and customer feedback.

### For Administrators

The admin panel helps keep the platform safe and organized.

- Approve or reject service provider accounts.
- Manage users, categories, services, and bookings.
- Handle reported reviews or inappropriate content.
- View platform statistics such as total users, providers, and bookings.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- User authentication and authorization using JWT.
- Three user roles: **Customer**, **Service Provider**, and **Admin**.
- Service provider registration and profile management.
- Service categories such as plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, tutoring, and repairs.
- Search, filter, and sort service providers.
- Booking system with booking status management.
- Ratings and reviews for service providers.
- Image upload for provider profiles and service portfolios.
- Admin dashboard for managing the platform.
- Responsive React frontend for mobile and desktop users.
